## Current best evidence-backed architecture

1. Formal V9 remains unchanged.
2. RSR1 and RSR2 remain separate, non-authorizing forward shadows starting with the first completed session on or after 2026-08-17.
3. No high-volatility, winner-extension, below-MA50 or delayed-core-exit module is added.
4. Cash yield is the only remaining capital-efficiency lever that does not require additional equity risk. The executable upper-bound scenario is 50%-60%, never 100%, and only when the expected idle period clears the account-specific break-even; this still needs broker and tax facts.
5. No additional parameter search on 2024–2026 current-list data is permitted merely to find a passing result.
6. A separate immutable opportunity-diagnostics ledger begins on 2026-08-17. It records high-volatility missed leaders and one-month core reversals but cannot authorize a trade or backfill the observed AAOI week.
7. No allocation challenger remains. The 80/20 core-only result did not survive shared-capital and whole-share testing; formal 70/30 stays unchanged without claiming that the current retrospective stock sample proves an optimum.
8. Forward review must report both hit rate and economic expectancy. A higher win rate cannot compensate for a lower payoff distribution or missing right-tail winners; the new scorecard is read-only and cannot authorize a trade.
9. Keep the current RS20-plus-volume ordering when qualified signals compete for capacity. RS-only and low-ATR-first materially reduced development return, while the balanced composite did not improve return or win rate; zero 2026 contention events prevent any alternative from becoming a forward challenger.
10. Do not add automatic half-position profit taking to RSR2. It improves retrospective drawdown and Sharpe slightly but lowers return and win rate by trimming persistent right-tail winners; released capacity can also admit an additional losing trade.
11. Genuine forward observation is active. The first four sessions through 2026-08-20 contain no RSR or matched-baseline signal and therefore no win-rate evidence. Opportunity diagnostics have 24 raw missed-leader observations/16 primary episodes, but no five-session outcome is mature; the new MRVL raw observation is not an independent episode and MRVL is already a real-account holding.
12. The exact point-in-time OHLCV audit closes the strongest available survivorship-bias follow-up without validating a hard filter. High coverage does not cure the cross-period failure: the quality pair improves some portfolio outcomes but does not deliver stable positive expectancy. Do not tune 4%/50% neighbors on this panel.
13. The current-list edge is not indistinguishable from a random best-of-20 result, but it is not proven either. Low PBO and a borderline family-wise p-value support keeping the frozen shadow; failure of the immutable 7/10 time-block gates prevents promotion. Do not delete inactive blocks or lower the gate after observation.
14. Interpret 20 closed trades correctly. It can falsify economics if payoff and expectancy fail, but it is underpowered to prove a majority hit rate or reproduce the historical headline. The original 20-trade/126-session gate remains unchanged; any 40-90 trade figures are confidence benchmarks, not silently added promotion requirements. Sparse arrival, not calendar time, is the binding constraint.
15. The historical research tree is frozen. All 26 registered branches are classified, the strongest point-in-time transfer follow-up failed, and the selection-bias concern remains uncontained. Further threshold, holding-period, ranking, allocation or universe searches on the same history are inadmissible; the next decisive evidence must be immutable forward sessions, closed trades and matured opportunity outcomes.

Missing-open exit orders are now preserved rather than silently dropped.
