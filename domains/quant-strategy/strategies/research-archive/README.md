# Research Archive

This folder preserves non-executable strategy work:

- `legacy-v0-v8-scripts/`: V0-V8 and V8.x strategy prototypes, optimizers and
  historical tests.
- `v9-variants-research/`: V9 validation studies and unpromoted V9.1 variants.
- dated folders and notes: historical backtests and institutional overlays.

None of these files may authorize a trade. The only execution entry is
[`../v9-execution`](../v9-execution/).
