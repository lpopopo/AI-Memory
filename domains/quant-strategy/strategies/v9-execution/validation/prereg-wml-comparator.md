# Pre-registration: WML Comparator

Frozen date: 2026-07-11  
Status: research comparator only. **V9 is not WML.**

## Purpose

Measure how cross-sectional momentum and its winner/loser legs behave across
panic-to-repair windows relative to SPY, QQQ and the V9 long-only stock sleeve.

## Tracks

1. Ken French UMD / reconstructed WML
2. Winner leg
3. Loser leg
4. SPY
5. QQQ
6. V9 information-stock sleeve (if any holdings; otherwise cash)

Horizons: 1 / 5 / 21 / 63 trading days after event dates.

## Required disclaimer

WML crash mechanics are dominated by the short loser leg. Any WML result may
inform V9 risk language but cannot validate MA trend weights or Rule E.

## Metrics

- mean / median forward returns by track
- skew and expected shortfall of WML
- fraction of crash windows driven by loser rebound
- coverage note when PIT membership or delisting returns are incomplete
